## Project Overview

Kajian ini membahas pendekatan perencanaan instalasi
pengolahan air limbah (IPAL) untuk mendukung pengelolaan
limbah cair pada kegiatan peternakan sapi perah.

## Background & Objectives

Pengelolaan air limbah peternakan membutuhkan sistem
pengolahan yang mempertimbangkan debit, karakteristik
limbah, beban pencemar, serta baku mutu lingkungan.

Tujuan kajian ini adalah mengevaluasi pendekatan
perencanaan IPAL berdasarkan metode perhitungan
dan kriteria desain yang relevan.

## Technical Approach

Pendekatan teknis meliputi:

- Identifikasi sumber dan debit air limbah.
- Perhitungan beban pencemar.
- Perencanaan unit pengolahan anaerobik.
- Perencanaan constructed wetland.
- Evaluasi pendekatan desain IPAL.

## Key Insights

Perencanaan IPAL perlu mempertimbangkan kesesuaian
antara karakteristik air limbah, kapasitas pengolahan,
kriteria desain, dan target baku mutu lingkungan.

## Project Overview

This study discusses the approach to planning a wastewater treatment plant (IPAL) to support liquid waste management at dairy cattle farming operations.

## Background & Objectives

Livestock wastewater management requires a treatment system that considers flow rate, wastewater characteristics, pollutant load, and environmental quality standards.

The objective of this study is to evaluate the IPAL planning approach based on relevant calculation methods and design criteria.

## Technical Approach

The technical approach covers:

- Identifying wastewater sources and flow rate.
- Calculating pollutant load.
- Planning anaerobic treatment units.
- Planning a constructed wetland.
- Evaluating the IPAL design approach.

## Key Insights

IPAL planning must consider the match between wastewater characteristics, treatment capacity, design criteria, and target environmental quality standards.
